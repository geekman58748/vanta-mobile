## 2. The X strategy, honestly

### 2.1 The uncomfortable truth about 2 followers

At 2 followers your posts reach approximately nobody, and no amount of posting cadence fixes that
because **X tests a post with a small slice of your existing followers first**; with no follower
pool there is nothing to test. Broadcasting is not a strategy at this stage — it is an investment
that pays off in 3 months, not 8 days.

What does work at low follower count, per the 2026 consensus: **replies on other people's threads**.
A thoughtful reply in a high-traffic thread is seen by everyone in that thread's audience, and it
is the only mechanism that bypasses your own follower graph entirely.

### 2.2 The 8-day reply discipline (30–45 min/day, not more)

Pick the 10–15 accounts whose audience is literally the judge panel. Reply *substantively*, not
"great post 🚀".

**Tier 1 — the judges and their orgs (reply within 15 min of any post)**
- `@solanamobile`, `@SolanaMobile` dev account, `@AkshayRajan`-style handles for the BD leads
- `@heliuslabs` — **highest value.** Anything Helius posts about Rings/Zolana: you have a shipped client. That is a real, relevant contribution, not engagement bait
- `@aeyakovenko` (Toly) — mostly protocol-level; only reply when you have a genuine technical observation
- `@0xMert_` (Mert) — Helius + market structure. Reply where Zolana/BREAKpoint/runtime comes up
- `@RadiantsDAO` — the co-host. They are running the Discord workshops. **Be in that Discord.** It is a room where the people judging you are reading

**Tier 2 — Solana mobile/consumer builders** who will amplify a real product demo

**Never** reply with a bare link. Lead with an observation or a number, let the profile do the work.
A reply that says "we shipped the first MWA client for Zolana on devnet — here's the vault delta on
today's shield: 49 → 59 dUSDC, slot 505745892" earns a click. A reply that says "check out our app"
does not.

### 2.3 Post format that survives muted autoplay

- **Native video, 20–40s, square or 9:16.** Muted-autoplay means the text on screen carries the
  entire message. Burn in captions or write on the frames; never rely on audio.
- **Lead with the number, not the pitch.** `"Public 50 → 40. Private 10."` is a hook. "Introducing
  Vanta, a privacy-first wallet" is not.
- **Threads beat singles** — roughly 3x impressions for a 3-tweet thread vs a standalone post.
- One idea per post. The product has four modes; do not explain all four in one tweet.
- Post in the 10am–4pm EST weekday band, and **reply to every comment within minutes** — reply depth
  is a ranking signal.

### 2.4 The demo-video-as-launch-content play

Your demo video is a required submission artifact **and** your best social asset. Cut it once into:

| Cut | Format | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Submission demo | 16:9 | 2–3 min | Hackathon submission |
| Launch film | 16:9 or 1:1 | 30–45s | Pinned tweet, landing page |
| Vertical | 9:16 | 20–30s | Shorts/Reels/TikTok |
| Loop | 1:1 | 6–10s | Reply attachment — the "watch this once" clip |

Zero extra creative work: it is one shoot, four renders.

## 3. The article: how to actually reach 5–10k views

**The hard truth:** with 2 followers, views come from **distribution**, not from writing. The
article is the destination; it is never the engine. Plan the distribution before you write a word.

### 3.1 Where to publish

| Platform | Why | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **X long-form / Article** | Native to where the goal-2 accounts live; linkless, so it doesn't get suppressed | Best for the "get seen" goal |
| **Hacker News (Show HN)** | One lucky front page = 5–50k reads in a day | Demo-first, no marketing speak. A privacy wallet with honest receipts and a real tx hash is *exactly* HN's taste |
| **dev.to / Hashnode** | Dev-credible, SEO-durable, cross-posts cheaply | Good canonical home |
| **Reddit** r/solana, r/solanamobile, r/privacy | Real traffic, harsh audience | Lead with the disclosure table, not the tagline |

Post the **same essay** everywhere; do not write five. One canonical URL, five doors.

### 3.2 The hook that earns the click

Do **not** open with "Introducing Vanta." Open with the receipt idea, because it is genuinely novel
and slightly uncomfortable — and discomfort is what stops a scroll:

> **"Every wallet tells you it's private. Here is a wallet that tells you exactly where it isn't."**

Then immediately back it with the disclosure table (slide 10 material) and the verified shield hash.
The honesty *is* the marketing: it is contrarian, it is falsifiable, and it gives critics nothing to
shoot at — which matters enormously in a comment section full of people who assume every privacy
claim is a lie.

### 3.3 Article skeleton

1. **Hook** — the quote above, 2 lines max
2. **The verified receipt** — sig `24QLsSqc…cxbRe`, slot 505745892, public 50→40, vault 49→59, `err: None`
3. **The thing nobody does** — the six residuals, stated plainly (this is the section people quote)
4. **Why mobile, why MWA** — key never enters the app; there is no desktop equivalent
5. **The honest limits** — small pool, reused spend identity, shared vault custody
6. **How to try it** — APK link + repo + faucet
7. **CTA** — "tell me which of the six leaks bothers you most" (invites replies, which is the metric)

### 3.4 Realistic view math

5–10k **article** views from a 2-follower account is achievable only through an outside channel.
Ranked by probability of actually happening in 8 days:

1. **Show HN hitting the front page** — highest ceiling, real chance given the honest-disclosure angle
2. **A single high-profile quote-tweet or reply** (a Helius or Solana Mobile account engaging)
3. **The hackathon itself** — judges reading it because they must
4. **Organic X reach at 2 followers** — negligible

So: optimize for #1 and #3, and treat #2 as a bonus. If you only do one distribution action, do
**Show HN with a live demo link and a clickable explorer URL**, not a tweet.

## 5. Angles worth using (ranked)

1. **"Helius built the rails, nobody built the phone."** Mert is judging. Lead with this.
2. **"The device deletes our only weakness."** The devnet build keeps the shielded key in
   `localStorage`; a Seeker puts it in the Seed Vault, where it cannot be extracted. The
   disclosed gap and the reason this is a Seeker app are the *same fact*. This matters more
   than it looks: the rubric **defines** PMF as "how well a project connects with the Solana
   Mobile and Seeker community", so this angle is scored, not decoration.
3. **"A wallet that tells you what it leaks."** Contrarian, quotable, defensible.
4. **"The receipt is the product."** Novel artifact; screenshots well.
5. **"Eight bugs a real device found."** Engineering credibility; HN and dev audiences love it.
6. **"1,333 lines of Kotlin behind an HTML UI."** Directly answers the PWA-wrapper suspicion before
   it is voiced — use it in replies where someone says "this is a website."
7. **The verified numbers.** 50→40 public, 49→59 vault, one program, `err: None`. Evidence travels.

### 5.1 The Seeker gap, and why more mentions are the wrong fix

Audited 2026-09-30. `grep -ri seeker` across the product:

| Area | Seeker footprint |
|---|---|
| `src/`, `landing/src/` | **One string** — an MWA-not-found error that says "or use a Seeker". The landing site does not name Seeker or Solana Mobile anywhere. |
| Android / Kotlin | **None.** No Seed Vault, no SMS SDK. |
| Seeker Genesis Token, SKR | **None.** No code. |
| `relayer/db.js` | `seeker`, `helius`, `solana`, `phantom`, `solflare` on the reserved `.handle` list, so nobody can impersonate them. |

So no, the gospel is not being preached — and the fix is **not** to sprinkle the word into the
UI. A technical judge clocks keyword stuffing instantly, and the repo's whole credibility rests on
not overclaiming.

The fix is framing, and the framing is already true: **Vanta's one disclosed weakness is the
`localStorage` key, and Seed Vault is what deletes it.** That is a real argument for this device,
it is worth up to 25% of the score, and it costs nothing to say because the README already
concedes the weakness.

What would be dishonest, and should not be attempted before Oct 8:

- **Claiming Seed Vault support.** It is the roadmap item and the README says so.
- **Gating anything on the Seeker Genesis Token** to appear Seeker-native. There is no SGT code,
  and one cannot be tested on an emulator — only the "not a Seeker" branch is reachable here.
- **Forcing an SKR use case** for the $10k side prize. Forced integration is detectable, and this
  panel is strong.

The genuine Seeker action available in the time left is **preparing the dApp Store listing**
(metadata, 512×512 icon, screenshots) — publishing there *is* the Seeker integration, and winners
are required to do it anyway.

## 6. What not to do

- 🚫 **Don't buy engagement, use pods, or bot followers.** In a judged hackathon with a security
  firm (EthelSec) on the panel, this is the one mistake that can disqualify the whole thing.
- 🚫 **Don't say "anonymous," "untraceable," or "no server."** `src/lib/honesty.js` exists precisely
  to stop the product contradicting itself; the marketing must not undo it.
- 🚫 **Don't claim mainnet.** It's devnet and the app says so. Say it first, before a judge does.
- 🚫 **Don't post a link without a number.** Links alone get ignored and suppressed.
- 🚫 **Don't let X eat the 8 days.** The demo video is a *required artifact*; a reply grind is not.
