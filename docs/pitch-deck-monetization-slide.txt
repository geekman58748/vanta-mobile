HOW VANTA MAKES MONEY
Model, not shipped. Devnet today, no fee taken.

THE CONSTRAINT IS THE BUSINESS MODEL
Zolana forbids a relayer from paying for a private spend. So we pay for
onboarding and deposits, and the user's own spend identity pays 5,000
lamports per send.

Onboarding costs us 0.0014 SOL per user — about 25 cents, once.
5,000 users costs about $1,400.
Break-even is roughly 20 private payments a day.

THREE WAYS WE EARN
- 0.5% on private sends, collected as a private fee note. Paid by the sender.
- Campaign plan: $49 + $1 per onboarded member. Paid by DAOs, grants, fundraisers.
- Relayer-as-a-service: $0.002 per call. Paid by other Seeker apps.

THE FEE IS PRIVATE TOO
The fee is split inside the same proof as the payment, so taking a cut can't
rebuild the graph the pool hides. Treasury exits are merged and swept, not
per user.


--- SAY THIS (30 sec) ---

Most privacy apps have a gas problem. A paymaster bleeds priority fees and a
backend swaps stablecoins back to SOL to stay alive. We don't, and it isn't
clever engineering, it's the SDK. The same guard that makes our spend identity
visible also means nobody can spend our money for us. So onboarding costs a
quarter, once, and everything after that is the user's own five thousand
lamports. We break even at about twenty private payments a day.

When we do charge, the fee rides the same proof the payment does. One note for
the recipient, one for us. We can't take a cut and stay honest about the graph
if that cut is a public transfer, so it isn't one.
