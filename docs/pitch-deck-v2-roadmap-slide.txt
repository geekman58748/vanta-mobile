THE UNLOCK: A CUSTOM RING
Today Zolana makes the note owner sign and pay for every spend. That is why our
spend identity is visible, and why the user needs SOL.
A custom Ring is Helius's supported extension point — the relayer becomes the
fee payer.

  · The spend identity disappears from the chain
  · The user never needs to hold SOL
  · And it ships with burner-funded deposits — otherwise we have only moved the leak

THEN, IN ORDER

  Custody              timelocked multisig authority · delete the localStorage
                       wallet · independent audit before mainnet

  Provider             local or fallback prover · RPC failover · push-based
  independence         note indexing (retires a whole bug class)

  Product              batch payout runs (a DAO pays 30 contributors privately) ·
                       payment links · receipts an auditor verifies without the app

  Sellable surface     an auditor-key compliance ring — so a DAO can buy privacy
                       and still answer its auditor

RULE
v2.0 is mainnet readiness with no new program. The ring is v2.1.
An unaudited program is a worse story than a working client.


--- SPEAKER NOTES (20 sec) ---

The honest version of our roadmap starts with the constraint we showed you.
Every spend today carries a reusable identity, because the SDK makes the note
owner pay. A custom Ring is Helius's own extension point — it lets our relayer
be the fee payer, and that identity goes away entirely.

But we won't ship it alone. Without a burner-funded deposit, relayer-paid spends
just move the leak from the spend to the on-ramp. Those two ship together or we
don't claim it. And an unaudited pool is a worse story than a working client, so
v2.0 is mainnet readiness with no new program, and the ring is v2.1.


--- IF ASKED ---

"Who pays for v2?"    Grants, then the Campaign plan (see the monetization slide).

"Why not build your own pool?"  Rings owns that surface. A custom Ring is the
                      supported extension point; writing our own circuits is a
                      multi-month unaudited build and a worse story, not a bolder one.

"What about Helius going down?"  Right now their prover and indexer are in the
                      liveness path — if they're down you can't see or spend your
                      notes. We say that out loud, and a fallback prover is on
                      this roadmap because of it.
